Chapter I

About

I am a final-year psychology student at The Hang Seng University of Hong Kong, specialising in educational and clinical psychology.

Outside the classroom, I tutor children one-to-one, write psychological content for the public, and support sleep research — and increasingly, I use AI tools in all three.

My intellectual home sits at the overlap of two loves: counselling psychology and technology. The writer I reread most is Hermann Hesse — fittingly, he was once a patient of Carl Jung, whose Red Book I first opened in high school and barely understood. I keep coming back.
